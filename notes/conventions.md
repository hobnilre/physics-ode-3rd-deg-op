# Conventions and exact configurations

All examples are predictions of declared ideal models. No hardware acquisition or numerical evolution is included. Time arguments in exponentials and trigonometric functions use the time unit specified with the configuration. Quantities called normalized are ratios to the stated physical scales. Roman numerals distinguish physical configurations, not entries in a research register.

Positive signed power enters the named boundary. Electrical effort/flow: voltage/current. Mechanical: force/velocity or torque/angular rate. Heat export has negative power; a thermal port uses boundary temperature times entropy carried by heat, with the opposite sign; internal entropy production is not this flow. Thermal storage is either retained or explicitly neglected. Each work is integrated independently before summation. End stores are evaluated from constitutive states. Numerical integration residual is identically zero; omitted physical laws and ports give an unevaluated model residual.

Colour map: blue `elemA` = plant, inertial/reactive stores; orange `elemB` = release, switching, loss and transfer channels; green `elemC` = external drive, controller or reference supply; purple `elemD` = internal observations or the second independently observed branch; grey `frameline` = boundary/fixture.
