# Conventions and exact configurations

All examples are predictions of declared ideal models. No hardware acquisition or numerical evolution is included. Time arguments in exponentials and trigonometric functions use the time unit specified with the configuration. Quantities called normalized are ratios to the stated physical scales. Roman numerals distinguish physical configurations, not entries in a research register.

Positive signed power enters the named boundary. Electrical effort/flow: voltage/current. Mechanical: force/velocity or torque/angular rate. Heat export has negative power; a thermal port can be written as temperature times outward entropy flow with the opposite sign. Each work is integrated independently before summation. End stores are evaluated from constitutive states. Numerical integration residual is identically zero; omitted physical laws and ports give an unevaluated model residual.

Colour map: blue `elemA` = plant, inertial/reactive stores; orange `elemB` = release, switching, loss and transfer channels; green `elemC` = external drive, controller or reference supply; purple `elemD` = internal observations or the second independently observed branch; grey `frameline` = boundary/fixture.

## Configurations

- I: oscillator/contact scales `m=1 kg`, `d=2 N s/m`, `k=2 N/m`, initial deformation zero, approaching velocity `1 m/s`, time unit `1 s`. For the mapped series circuit, `alpha=1 C/m`, `beta=1 V/N`, hence `L=1 H`, `R=2 ohm`, `C=1/2 F`. Preparation is `x=t(t+1)^2 m` for numerical time `-1 <= t <= 0`; free response `x=exp(-t) sin(t) m`. Finite reset lasts `1 s` from `T=pi/2 s`. The bilateral oscillator is kept connected during reset; a unilateral collision instead obeys its declared release law.
- II: static torsional ambiguity, drive torque `1 N m`; `(k_c,k_j)=(2,2)` or `(3,3/2) N m/rad`. Hammer angle `1 rad`; anvil angle `1/2` or `2/3 rad`. Inertias and dampers are positive but vanish from this static balance.
- III: dimensionless coefficient `b=w0+w1 eta+w2 eta^2/rho`, weights `(1,1/100,1/2)`; alternative `(101/100,0,1/2)`. Coordinates `(rho,eta)=(1,1),(4,1),(1,2)`. Correlated observation covariance is defined, never assumed known experimentally.
- IV: illustrative resonator `L_m=1 H`, `C_m=1 F`, `R_m=1 ohm`; shunt `C_0=1 F` or `2 F`, `omega=1 rad/s`, peak voltage `1 V`. These are normalized low-frequency component values, not quartz specifications. Physical quartz uses the same symbolic admittance with calibrated parameters.
- V: polarization branches each `C=1 mF`, `R=1 kohm`, hidden voltages `+1 V,-1 V`; observation time `ln(2) s`, terminal current zero. Equal and opposite preparation ramps last `1 s` and use separately accessible branch sources.
- VI: absorber pair with `F=1 N` peak, `omega=10 sqrt(10) rad/s`, `(m,k2,A)=(1/10 kg,100 N/m,1 mm^2)` or `(1/5 kg,200 N/m,2 mm^2)`. Primary `M=1 kg`, support `k0=100 N/m,c0=1 N s/m`, fixed base, coupling damping zero. The two couplings have the same elastic modulus and length and twice the area/stiffness in the second device. Prepared periodic state; no assertion of attraction in zero coupling damping.
- VII: singular prepared RC family `C_epsilon=epsilon*1 mF`, initial voltage `2/sqrt(epsilon) V`, finite resistor `1 kohm`; compare durations with `h=T/(R C_epsilon)` fixed. Worked finite member `epsilon=1/4`, `h=ln(2)`.
- VIII: two capacitors `C1=C2=1 mF`, initial voltages `2 V,0 V`. Difference store `D=1 mJ`, common store `1 mJ`. Each channel action is `K=ln(2)`; reset duration `epsilon>0`, with the permanent post resistor disconnected until its end. Conductance profiles each have unit integral in normalized time.
- IX: coupled clamp `L1=L2=1/10 H`, `M=1/20 H`, `C=1/750 F`, `V_c=1 V`, initial primary current `pi/10 A`, secondary current and capacitor voltage zero. `omega=100 rad/s`; first primary zero `pi/100 s`. All winding losses omitted in this exact control. Reversing the sign of `M` reverses secondary state signs.
- X: source-off transducer `L=m=1/10` in H and kg, `R=c=1/2` in ohm and N s/m, `k=5 N/m`, `g_e=1/2 V s/m`, `g_m=-1 N/A`. States `i=exp(-10t)/10 A`, `v=exp(-10t)/10 m/s`, `x=-exp(-10t)/100 m`, interval `0 <= t <= ln(2)/10 s`. Reciprocal baseline `g=g_e`; separate commanded actuator force `(g_m-g_e)i`. Regenerative efficiency `eta=4/5` is illustrative and constant. Bias `Lb=1/10 H`, `Rb=1 ohm`, `ib(0)=1/100 A`. DC link `Cd=1 mF`, `Vd(0)=5 V`, constant logic demand `1 mW`, charger off. Dissipative alternative has `eta=0` with the same plant force command. No claim of similarity to an entire unstable numerical history; the exact finite trajectory is a separate, instrumentable model.
- XI: accelerating-reference cell `J=1/10 kg m^2`, `alpha=1 V/(rad/s)`, `C=1/10 F`; `omega(0)=Omega(0)=0`, torque zero, `Omega=2t rad/s`, interval `[0,1] s`. Driver `Cd=1/200 F`, `Rd=3/4 ohm`, `u=2t+3/400 V`. Additional reversal control has zero reference acceleration and `tau=J(1-2t)`, returning to zero rate at `1 s`. Illustrative source conversion efficiency is either one or `4/5`, separate from transducer efficiency.

The full free-pair extension of I uses `m1=m2=2 kg` (reduced mass `1 kg`) and voltage/velocity scale `alpha_v=1 V/(m/s)`: two grounded `2 F` capacitors connected by `L_c=1/2 H` in parallel with `G_c=2 S`. Initial body velocities are `+1/2,-1/2 m/s`; opposite body drives `(f,-f)` leave the center-of-mass rate zero. The two body ports and the contact store are all retained.

No article result depends on a value inferred from an energy residual. Switching constraints precede any energy calculation. Variable component families mean different fixed components on different finite intervals, not an unaccounted parameter actuator.
